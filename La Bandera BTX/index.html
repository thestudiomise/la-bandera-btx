The Roots

Straight from Tijuana to the Heart of the 956

Established in 2021, La Bandera BTX wasn't born to blend in. We brought the raw, authentic essence of Tijuana-style beef birria to Brownsville, Texas. No shortcuts. Just pure, slow-cooked dedication, melted cheese crusts, and spice-infused consomé that makes every bite unforgettable.

We believe birria is a craft. From our custom paper wrap to our selection of loaded signature creations, we serve visual masterpieces that taste even better than they look.

The Craft

Our Legendary Lineup

Viral Favorite

Cheeto Birria Bombs

Crunchy, cheesy, and absolutely legendary. Shredded beef birria bombs rolled in Flamin' Hot Cheeto crumbs, fried golden, and served with a syringe of melted warm cheese and signature consomé.

Tijuana Fusion

Birria Ramen

East meets West. Warm ramen noodles submerged in a deeply seasoned beef consomé broth, topped with massive heaps of tender beef birria, fresh onions, cilantro, and lime.

Crowd Pleaser

Quesabirria Pizza

A giant double-decker quesabirria cut in pizza wedges. Loaded with melted mozzarella, succulent birria, fresh onions, and cilantro, topped with a massive scoop of fresh guacamole.

Flavor Bomb

Birria Dumplings

Steamed and seared gyoza-style dumplings packed with our juicy birria de res. Swimming in warm consomé, drizzled with homemade chili oil, sesame seeds, chopped onions, and cilantro.

Huge Portions

El Gigante Burrito

A massive flour tortilla toasted to a golden crisp. Stuffed to the brim with slow-cooked birria, melted cheese, onions, and cilantro. Served with a lime wedge and double-dip red and green salsas.

Sweet & Spicy

Loaded Aguas Frescas

Perfect heat-quenchers. Ice-cold Mango or Watermelon aguas frescas with a thick chamoy and Tajín rim, stacked high with fresh diced fruit, pineapple chunks, and a maraschino cherry.

Brunch Legend

Chilaquiles Divinos

Crispy hand-cut tortilla chips drenched in rich red salsa. Topped with shredded birria de res, two perfect sunny-side-up eggs, crema, fresh cilantro, microgreens, and pickled red onions.

The Branding

It's All in the Wrap

Our signature abstract parchment paper is a stamp of quality. Just like our food, the wrap is custom, bold, and unapologetically local. It carries the code of the 956 and the legacy of Tijuana's street food scene.
